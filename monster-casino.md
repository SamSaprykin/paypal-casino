### Verifying Your Account (KYC)

Monster Casino requires identity verification (KYC) to meet its licensing obligations and to protect your account — your account may be limited from depositing or withdrawing until it's complete, so it's worth doing early rather than waiting until your first cash-out. Documents are uploaded from the Cashier tab under "Verify ID" as PDF, JPEG or PNG files, shown in full, uncropped, and in colour. You'll typically need:

- **Photo ID** — a valid driving licence or passport, shown in full and not blurry.
- **Proof of address** — a utility bill (not a phone bill), council tax bill, or bank statement no older than 3 months; electronic PDF copies are accepted.
- **Proof of payment** — for a card, a photo of the front and back with the middle 8 digits and CVV covered; for an eWallet, a screenshot showing your name and email together; for a mobile number, a recent phone bill or a screenshotted live-chat confirmation from your provider for pay-as-you-go accounts.
